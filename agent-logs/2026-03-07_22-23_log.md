### Root causes:

1. **Sphere overgrowth into cylinder regions** — The biggest problem. On a rounded cube, every cylindrical fillet has the same radius (r=2) as the corner spheres. The cylinder fillet faces genuinely lie on a sphere of radius 2 centered at the nearest corner. BFS sphere growth eats nearly ALL fillet faces — in the fine mesh, each sphere claims ~1300 faces (95% of all faces!), when the actual spherical cap at each corner is much smaller.

2. **Bogus tilted cylinders on sphere faces** — With finer meshes, faces on spherical corners produce many spurious cylinder hypotheses with tilted axes (e.g., axis=(0.95, 0, 0.31)). These pass the angular coverage check because sphere faces do span circumferentially. In the fine mesh, 84+ of 91 cylinders are bogus.

3. **Cylinder fragmentation** — Even the legitimate cylinders fragment into multiple smaller hypotheses because sphere overgrowth claims most of their faces first, leaving gaps that prevent BFS from growing complete cylinders.

4. **Coplanarity in fine mesh** — The fine mesh has 71 multi-face planar hypotheses (faces on the fillet that are so finely tessellated they appear coplanar). These block cylinder seeding.

The fundamental issue is that stage 2.6's greedy area-based selection picks the 8 massive sphere hypotheses first (they have the most area), leaving only scraps for cylinders. The sphere BFS in stage 2.3 needs to be constrained to not grow
