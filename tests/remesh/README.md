# OCCT re-meshes of existing references (Phase 0.2)

Generated by `gen-test-models remesh` — see the "Re-mesh mode" section of
`src/bin/gen_test_models.rs` for the settings and the reasoning. Regenerate
with:

```bash
cargo run --release --bin gen-test-models -- remesh tests/remesh
```

The mesh output is deterministic: re-running produces byte-identical STLs.
The `*_offset.step` files differ only in the timestamp OCCT embeds in the
STEP header, exactly as the other committed reference STEPs do.

## What these add

Every model here is a **re-tessellation of a reference STEP that already
exists elsewhere in `tests/`**, so the ground truth is unchanged and *only the
mesh provenance* varies. Two properties change, both of which the pipeline had
quietly been able to rely on:

* **Constrained Delaunay instead of strips.** The Onshape (Parasolid) exports
  tessellate a swept surface as full-length quads with no interior vertices
  along the ruling. OCCT's `BRepMesh` produces Delaunay triangles instead. A
  facet therefore no longer spans two rulings of a cylinder, so its normal is
  no longer exactly perpendicular to the axis, and facets are not grid-aligned,
  so torus meridian normals are only approximate. Both are load-bearing
  assumptions for the Phase 1 closed-form initializers.
* **Off-origin coordinates.** An STL stores f32, so vertex precision is
  relative: `max|coordinate| × 1.19e-7`. The `*_offset*` variants are
  translated ~1 m (600, 800, 0 mm), which raises the quantum from ~3e-6 mm to
  ~1e-4 mm — past the 5e-5 mm default `--vertex-tolerance`.

## Naming

* `<model>_occt_{coarse,medium,fine}.stl` — deflection 0.5 / 0.1 / 0.02 mm at
  30° / 15° / 6°. Compare against the ORIGINAL reference (e.g.
  `tests/onshape/cylinder_10x30.step`); no copy is kept here.
* `<model>_offset_occt_medium.stl` + `<model>_offset.step` — the medium
  settings applied to the translated body, with its own translated reference.

Every mesh is a closed oriented manifold; tessellation volume error runs from
7.3e-6 (fine plate) to 4.65e-2 (coarse sphere) relative.

**The off-origin meshes pass stage 1 `--compare` with 2.7e-5 to 4.2e-5 mm max
vertex-to-surface distance** — under the 5e-5 default, but only just, and
about 0.42× their own quantum. The quantum bounds a coordinate error; the
distance to the surface is that error projected onto the normal, so half a
quantum is the operative figure. At ~800 mm from the origin the default
vertex tolerance therefore has under 20% margin, and a part positioned a few
metres out in an assembly would break it. That is what Phase 0.6's clamp is
for.

## Status against the reference (see tests/stage4_integration.rs)

25 of the 32 pass end-to-end `--compare`. The 7 that fail are all real
findings, not corpus defects, and carry precise blockers in the suite:

* `stepped_block_toroidal` fails at **every** density including fine, while
  the Parasolid fine mesh of the same body passes at exactly 71 surfaces. The
  18 tori are found every time; the planes and cylinders around them
  fragment (88 surfaces at medium, 97 at fine). This is the single most
  valuable row in this directory: it is the strip assumption showing up as a
  measurable failure rather than as a suspicion.
* Coarse `sphere_25`, `rounded_cube_10_r2` and `cone_tangent_planes` fail;
  their medium and fine variants pass.
